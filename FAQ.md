# FAIZAI - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 📖 General Questions

### What is FAIZAI?

FAIZAI (formerly TutorAI) is an AI-powered classroom assistant designed specifically for B.Sc. Computer Science students at Mazharul Uloom College. It provides intelligent tutoring, study tools, exam preparation, and administrative features.

### Who developed FAIZAI?

FAIZAI was developed by **Mohammed Faiz** in collaboration with AI technologies. It's a college project that has evolved into a comprehensive educational platform.

### What makes FAIZAI different from other educational apps?

- **Syllabus-Aware**: AI understands your specific college curriculum
- **Comprehensive**: 11 student features + 8 admin features in one platform
- **Real-time**: Live notifications and updates
- **Code Help**: Specialized assistance for programming labs
- **Free Access**: No subscription fees for college students
- **Privacy-Focused**: Data stays within your college ecosystem

## 🎓 For Students

### How do I access FAIZAI?

1. Visit the FAIZAI website
2. Click "Login"
3. Enter your Roll Number and Access Code
4. Access your personalized dashboard

Your roll number and access code are provided by your college administration.

### What can I do with FAIZAI?

You have access to 11 features:

1. **Ask AI** - Chat with AI tutor about any topic
2. **Quiz Generator** - Create and take quizzes
3. **Exam Strategy** - Get exam preparation materials
4. **Lab Assistant** - Get programming help
5. **Study Planner** - 7-day personalized schedules
6. **Syllabus Browser** - Explore your curriculum
7. **Notifications** - College announcements
8. **Notes & Files** - Course materials
9. **Naan Mudhalvan** - Government courses
10. **Extra Courses** - Additional resources
11. **My Profile** - Manage your information

### Is my chat history saved?

Yes! All your conversations with the AI are automatically saved. You can:
- View past conversations
- Continue old conversations
- Create new conversation threads
- Each conversation is titled automatically

### How does the AI know my syllabus?

The AI has been configured with the complete B.Sc. Computer Science syllabus for all 6 semesters. When you ask questions, it references this syllabus to provide accurate, curriculum-aligned answers.

### Can I use FAIZAI for exam preparation?

Absolutely! Use the **Exam Strategy** feature:
1. Enter your subject
2. Get 5 likely exam questions
3. Receive revision notes
4. Study a model answer

This helps you understand what to expect and how to answer effectively.

### Does the Lab Assistant write my code for me?

The Lab Assistant is designed to **help you learn**, not just give answers:
- It generates working code examples
- Provides detailed explanations
- Helps you understand the logic
- Assists with debugging

**Remember**: The goal is to help you learn programming, not to bypass learning!

### How accurate are the AI-generated quizzes?

Quizzes are generated by Google's Gemini AI based on your subject. They are:
- Generally accurate for standard topics
- Good for self-assessment
- Should be supplemented with official study materials
- Useful for testing your understanding

### Can I access FAIZAI on my phone?

Yes! FAIZAI is fully responsive and works on:
- Desktop computers
- Laptops
- Tablets
- Smartphones

The interface adapts to your screen size for the best experience.

### Is FAIZAI free?

Yes, FAIZAI is provided **free of charge** to all B.Sc. Computer Science students at Mazharul Uloom College. There are no subscription fees or in-app purchases.

### What happens to my data?

Your data is stored securely in Firebase (Google's cloud platform):
- Chat history
- Quiz results
- Study plans
- Profile information

Only you can access your personal data. Admins cannot see your chat history or quiz performance.

## 👨‍💼 For Administrators

### How do I access the admin portal?

1. Visit the FAIZAI website
2. Click "Login"
3. Switch to "Admin Login"
4. Enter your admin email and password (Firebase Authentication)

### What can admins do?

Admins have 8 management features:

1. **Setup & Data** - Initial bulk uploads
2. **Students** - Manage student accounts
3. **Notifications** - Push announcements
4. **Notes** - Upload course materials
5. **Syllabus** - Update curriculum
6. **Naan Mudhalvan** - Manage government courses
7. **Extra Courses** - Add supplementary resources
8. **Access Codes** - View student codes

### How do I add a new student?

1. Login as admin
2. Go to "Students" section
3. Click "Add Student"
4. Fill in: Roll Number, Name, Email, Phone
5. An access code is automatically generated
6. Share the roll number and code with the student

### How do I push a notification to all students?

1. Go to "Notifications" section
2. Click "Add Notification"
3. Type your message
4. Click "Send"

All students will see the notification in real-time in their dashboard!

### Can I edit the syllabus?

Yes! Go to "Syllabus" section:
1. Select semester
2. Edit the JSON data
3. Save changes

Be careful when editing - invalid JSON will cause errors.

### How do I upload course notes?

1. Go to "Notes" section
2. Click "Add Note"
3. Enter title and link (URL to your uploaded file/document)
4. Save

Students can then access these notes from their dashboard.

### Can I see students' chat history?

**No.** Student chat conversations are private. Admins cannot access:
- Individual chat messages
- Quiz results
- Lab solutions
- Study plans

This is by design to protect student privacy.

### How do I bulk upload students?

Use the **Setup & Data** feature (one-time setup):
1. Go to "Setup & Data"
2. Click "Bulk Upload Students"
3. This uploads 30 predefined students

**Note**: This is designed for initial setup only. For adding individual students later, use the "Students" section.

## 💻 Technical Questions

### What technology is FAIZAI built with?

**Frontend:**
- Next.js 15 (React framework)
- TypeScript (type-safe JavaScript)
- Tailwind CSS (styling)
- shadcn/ui (component library)

**Backend:**
- Next.js Server Actions
- Firebase Firestore (database)
- Firebase Authentication

**AI:**
- Google Genkit framework
- Gemini 2.0 Flash model

### How does the AI work?

FAIZAI uses **Google Genkit** to create structured AI workflows:

1. Your question is sent to a server action
2. The server action invokes a Genkit "flow"
3. The flow constructs a prompt with context (syllabus, history)
4. The prompt is sent to Google's Gemini AI
5. The AI generates a response
6. The response is validated and returned to you

### Is the AI always accurate?

The AI (Google Gemini 2.0 Flash) is:
- ✓ Very accurate for standard academic topics
- ✓ Good at explaining concepts
- ✓ Helpful for code generation
- ⚠️ Occasionally makes mistakes
- ⚠️ May not have the latest information

**Always verify important information with your textbooks and professors!**

### What programming languages does the Lab Assistant support?

The Lab Assistant can generate code in:
- **Python** (Data structures, general programming)
- **C++** (Object-oriented programming)
- **Java** (Advanced programming)
- **SQL** (Database queries)

### How is my password stored?

**Students**: You don't have a password! You login with your roll number and access code. The access code is stored as plain text in the database (it's a shared secret, not a password).

**Admins**: Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication, which uses industry-standard security:
- Passwords are hashed (not stored in plain text)
- Managed by Google's secure infrastructure
- Cannot be retrieved, only reset

### Can I run FAIZAI locally?

Yes! See the [QUICK_START.md](QUICK_START.md) guide. You'll need:
1. Node.js 20+
2. Firebase account
3. Google AI API key

### Is the code open source?

The code is available on GitHub, but the license terms should be clarified by the developer. Currently, it's developed for educational use at Mazharul Uloom College.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### I forgot my access code. What do I do?

Contact your college administrator. They can:
- Look up your access code in the admin portal
- Regenerate a new code if needed

### The AI is not responding. What's wrong?

Possible causes:
1. **Internet connection** - Check your network
2. **API quota exceeded** - Contact admin
3. **Server error** - Try again later
4. **Invalid input** - Check your question format

### I can't see new notifications. Why?

Try:
1. Refresh the page
2. Click on "Notifications" to mark as read
3. Check your internet connection
4. Clear browser cache

### The quiz won't generate. Help!

Troubleshooting steps:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Try a different subject
3. Reduce the number of questions
4. Refresh the page
5. Try again in a few minutes

### Chat messages aren't saving. What's happening?

This usually means:
1. Firestore connection issue
2. Permission problem
3. Network interruption

Try:
- Refresh the page
- Logout and login again
- Check internet connection
- Contact admin if persists

### The app is slow. How can I improve performance?

Tips for better performance:
1. **Use Chrome/Firefox** - Best compatibility
2. **Clear browser cache** - Old data can slow things down
3. **Check internet speed** - 3G minimum, 4G recommended
4. **Close other tabs** - Free up memory
5. **Update browser** - Use latest version

### Error: "Firebase API key is invalid"

This is a setup issue. The admin needs to:
1. Check Firebase configuration
2. Verify environment variables
3. Ensure API key is correct

Contact your system administrator.

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

### Is my data secure?

Yes! Security measures include:
- **Encryption**: All data transmitted over HTTPS
- **Firebase Security**: Industry-standard cloud security
- **Access Control**: Role-based permissions
- **Session-based**: Auth clears on browser close
- **Private Data**: Chat history is user-scoped

### Who can see my information?

- **Your Profile**: Only you and admins
- **Chat History**: Only you
- **Quiz Results**: Only you
- **Study Plans**: Only you
- **Notifications**: All students (broadcast)

### Does Google see my data?

When you use AI features:
- Your questions are sent to Google's Gemini API
- Google processes them to generate responses
- According to Google's terms, they don't use your data to train models
- Data is processed temporarily, not stored long-term

### Can I delete my data?

Currently, there's no self-service data deletion. Contact your admin to:
- Delete your account
- Remove your chat history
- Clear your quiz results

### Is there a privacy policy?

A formal privacy policy should be added by the institution. Key points should cover:
- Data collection practices
- Data usage and storage
- Third-party services (Firebase, Google AI)
- User rights and data deletion
- Contact information

## 🎓 Academic Integrity

### Is using FAIZAI cheating?

**No**, when used properly! FAIZAI is a **learning tool**, like:
- Textbooks
- YouTube tutorials
- Study groups
- Office hours

**Proper Use:**
- ✓ Understanding concepts
- ✓ Practicing problems
- ✓ Exam preparation
- ✓ Clarifying doubts

**Improper Use:**
- ✗ Copying code directly for submissions
- ✗ Using during closed-book exams
- ✗ Claiming AI output as your own work
- ✗ Bypassing actual learning

### Can I use AI-generated code in assignments?

**Guidelines:**
1. **Check with your professor** - Some may allow it, some may not
2. **Understand the code** - Don't just copy-paste
3. **Cite your source** - Mention if AI-assisted
4. **Learn from it** - Use it as a learning tool

**General Rule**: If you can explain every line of the code, you've learned!
